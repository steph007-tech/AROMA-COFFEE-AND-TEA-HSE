Our Company

Aroma Coffee and tea house was established in the year 1889 with an aim of bringing quality tea and coffee products to our customers.The company was founded out of passion for warm hospitality and for the rich tea and coffee cultuure of Kenya.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to provide high-quality tea and coffee products at an affordable price to our customers while offering excellent customer service creating a welcoming environment to every customer.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to become a trusted coffee and tea brand in Kenya and beyond known for quality products,customer satisfaction and responsible sourcing.
